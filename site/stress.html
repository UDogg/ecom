Thousands of shoppers hit the shop at once while a red team tries to reach the core services without the BFF — unsigned, with forged signatures, back-dated, with a fake privileged token, and by knocking on ports that aren’t there. Every attack is refused. This page models the real arena (stress/arena.py); run it for real with ./run.sh stress.

▶ Simulation. This runs entirely in your browser — no backend to call on a static site — reproducing the behaviour and rules of the real stress test.
